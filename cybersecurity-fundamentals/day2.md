# Cybersecurity Learning Log

## Day 2

### Types of attackers
#### Amateurs 
- Script kiddies
  > People that usually find ways to launch an attack by using tools or instructions found on the internet, but usually don't understand how they work
#### Hackers
- White hat attackers
  > They're the ones that break into networks or computer systems in order to identify vulnerabilities so that the security system can be improved (done with permission and results reported back to the owner)
- Gray hat attackers
  > Also look for vulnerabilities but without having permission and they usually do it to test their skills or even just for fun, in general their reasons may vary
- Black hat attackers
  > These attackers exploit weaknesses for malicious or unauthorized purposes, such as financial theft, stealing information, or disrupting systems
#### Organized hackers 
- organizations of cyber criminals, hacktivists, terrorists and state-sponsored hackers
  > They're usually highly skilled and precise and may even provide cybercrime as a service to other criminals. Hacktivists make political statements to create awareness about issues that are important to them. State-sponsored attackers gather intelligence or commit sabotage on behalf of their government.
